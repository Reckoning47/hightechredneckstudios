Splash — Delete your data

short version: it's all on your phone, and you can wipe it in two taps

There is no account to delete

Splash has no accounts, no login, and no server of ours. We never receive a copy of your plants, photos, notes, or reminders — so there is nothing on our end for us to delete on your behalf. Everything lives in the app's private storage on your own device, which means you can remove all of it yourself, immediately, without asking anyone.

Delete everything at once

Either of these removes all Splash data from your device:

  • In the app: tap the gear icon → scroll to Danger zone → Wipe all data. This cancels every scheduled reminder and deletes all plants, photos, cached AI results, saved diagnoses, and your saved API key. There is no undo.
  • Uninstall Splash. Android removes the app's private storage along with it.

Delete specific things

  • One plant — long-press it in the list, or open it and tap Delete plant. This also cancels that plant's reminders and removes its photo.
  • One reminder — open the plant and tap the ✕ on that reminder.
  • Your Gemini API key — Settings → Clear saved key. This disables the optional AI features and removes the key from your device's secure storage.

Two things we cannot delete for you

Being straight with you about the limits, both of which are covered in more detail in the privacy policy:

  • Photos and plant names you sent to Google's Gemini API — if you used "Identify with AI" or "Diagnose plant", that content went to Google under your own API key, and Google's retention policies govern it. Splash cannot reach into Google's systems. Manage it through Google My Activity and Google's privacy tools, or delete the API key's project in Google AI Studio.
  • Export files you already shared — if you used Settings → Export plants and sent the file somewhere (cloud drive, email, another phone), those copies are wherever you put them. Wiping the app does not touch them; delete them yourself at the destination.
