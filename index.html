The shop

High Tech Redneck Studios is a one-person workshop for personal software projects — built with practical taste, the kind of stuff you'd cobble together in a barn if barns had IDEs. Tools made because they were needed. Kept simple on purpose.

No analytics. No accounts. No ads. No funny business. Just things that work, and a person on the other end who actually answers email.

House rules
